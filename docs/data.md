# Data credit

The main speech and transcripts come from the **ATCO2 corpus**, using the free
one-hour subset `ATCO2-test-set-1h` (download name `ATCO2-ASRdataset-v1_beta`),
which the ATCO2 project makes available for research purposes at
<https://www.atco2.org/data>. One script also reads the ATCO2 language-ID
dataset, but nothing downstream uses its output.

> Zuluaga-Gomez, J., Veselý, K., Szöke, I., Motlicek, P., et al. *ATCO2 corpus:
> A Large-Scale Dataset for Research on Automatic Speech Recognition and Natural
> Language Understanding of Air Traffic Control Communications.*
> arXiv:2211.04054, 2022.

How the ATCO2 data is used here:

- The one-hour set was published as a **test set**. This project splits it
  80/20 into its own training and evaluation clips (699 / 175), so its scores
  cannot be compared with published ATCO2 results.
- The split was made per clip, not per recording: 101 of the 175 evaluation
  clips come from recordings that also supplied training clips
  ([details](evaluation.md)).
- Entity labels are **not** from ATCO2's annotations. They are generated by the
  word-list rules in [training/entity_labels.py](../training/entity_labels.py).

## UWB-ATCC, for the 2026 retraining

One of the 2026 models also trains on the training split of the **UWB-ATCC
corpus**: 11,291 clips, 10.5 hours of real controller and pilot speech from Czech
airspace, transcribed by hand. It is used for training only; every score is on
ATCO2 clips. It is published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 at
<https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jzuluaga/uwb_atcc>, and
[training/prepare_uwb_atcc.py](../training/prepare_uwb_atcc.py) turns it into
clips with ATCO2-style spelling.

> Šmídl, L., Švec, J., Tihelka, D., Matoušek, J., Romportl, J. and Ircing, P.
> *Air traffic control communication (ATCC) speech corpora and their use for ASR
> and TTS development.* Language Resources and Evaluation 53, 449–464, 2019.

## What is not in this repository

No audio, transcripts or model weights are included in this repository. To run
the evaluation, download the corpus from the ATCO2 site and prepare it with the
scripts in `experiments/asr/01_data_preparation/` and `training/`.
